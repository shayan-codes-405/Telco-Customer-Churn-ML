⚠️ Support Friction & Plan Dynamics
How customer care escalations and service add-ons impact carrier attrition.
🎧 Customer Support Calls vs Churn Rate
Critical Escalation
📊 Graph Explanation & Insight:
Customers with 0 to 3 calls have a flat, safe churn rate of ~10.9%. However, at 4 calls, churn surges to 44.0%, reaching 67.9% at 6 calls.
💡 Strategic Takeaway: 4 support calls represents the critical tipping point. Implementing an automated supervisory alert on the 3rd support call will catch at-risk accounts before they cancel.
✈️ Subscription Add-on Plan Impact
Commercial Pricing
📊 Graph Explanation & Insight:
Subscribers with an active International Plan experience 42.2% churn (vs 11.2% without). Conversely, subscribers with Voicemail have only 7.4% churn (vs 16.4% without).
💡 Strategic Takeaway: International callers suffer from high out-of-bundle rates causing bill shock. Bundling free voicemail and discount international calling packs directly stabilizes retention.
